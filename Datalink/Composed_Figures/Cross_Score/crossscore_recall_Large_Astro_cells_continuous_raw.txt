Cross-method recall for |LFC| ≥ 3 genes (Astrocytes, continuous panels). Rows are the DE method whose downsampled calls are being scored, columns are the DE method providing the full-data truth set. Cell values (discrete panels) are recall percentages. The cyan contour marks the 80% threshold. Diagonal panels (cyan outline) are self-scored and reproduce the standard within-method recall grid. Y axis: Cells per Donor.
