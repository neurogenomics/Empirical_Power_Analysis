Cross-method precision for |LFC| 0.2–1 genes (Astrocytes, discrete panels). Rows are the DE method whose downsampled calls are being scored, columns are the DE method providing the full-data truth set. Cell values (discrete panels) are precision percentages: of the genes each method calls with its OWN estimated |LFC| in the |LFC| 0.2–1 band, the fraction that lie in the column method's full truth set (all bins) — the standard per-bin precision used elsewhere in the paper. Cyan tile outlines marks the 80% threshold. Diagonal panels (cyan outline) are self-scored. Y axis: Cells per Donor.
