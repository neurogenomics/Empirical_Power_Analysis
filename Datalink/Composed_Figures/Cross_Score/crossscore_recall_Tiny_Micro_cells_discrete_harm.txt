Cross-method harmonised recall for |LFC| 0.2–1 genes (Microglia, discrete panels). Rows are the DE method whose downsampled calls are being scored, columns are the DE method providing the full-data truth set. Cell values (discrete panels) are recall percentages; cyan tile outlines marks the 80% threshold. Diagonal panels (cyan outline) are self-scored and reproduce the standard within-method recall grid. Y axis: Cells per Donor.
