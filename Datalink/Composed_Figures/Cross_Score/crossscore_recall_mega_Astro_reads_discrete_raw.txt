Cross-method recall in Astrocytes (discrete panels; target reads per donor). Each outer cell shows one (subsample method, truth method) pair: rows are the DE method whose downsampled calls are being scored, columns are the DE method providing the full-data truth set. Each inner 2x2 shows the four |LFC| bins (top row: |LFC| >= 3 and |LFC| 2-3; bottom row: |LFC| 1-2 and |LFC| 0.2-1). Colour scale is recall on [0, 1] (magma). Cyan panel outlines mark self-scored diagonals. Cyan cell outlines marks the 80% recall threshold. Y axis: Target Counts per Donor; X axis: number of donors. Grey cells: missing or non-computable grid points.
